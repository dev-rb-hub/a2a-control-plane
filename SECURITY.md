## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for suspected vulnerabilities.

Report them privately through GitHub: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/dev-rb-hub/a2a-control-plane/security/advisories/new).

Include the affected component, steps to reproduce and the impact you observed. Do not include real personal or payment data.
